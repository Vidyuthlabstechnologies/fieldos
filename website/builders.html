FAQ

Questions firms ask before they switch

Do my engineers need to subscribe in Google Play?

No. Once your company plan is switched on, everyone who joins your company in FieldOS with its join code gets in. They install the free app and sign in.

What counts as an active site?

Any site in FieldOS that is not marked completed, including sites on hold. Finished a site? Mark it completed and the slot is free for the next one.

How do we pay?

Yearly, by bank transfer against a GST invoice from ML Dynamic Solutions Private Limited. There is no card or app-store payment for company plans.

What happens to our records if we stop?

They stay on your phones and are never deleted when a plan ends. You can export everything to Excel at any time.

We already use Excel and WhatsApp. Is moving hard?

Our team imports your current workers, rates and BOQ, and sits with your supervisors on site for the first days. Most sites send their first full daily report in the first week.

Is there an iPhone app?

FieldOS runs on Android today, and the iPhone app is on its way. Company plans cover both.
