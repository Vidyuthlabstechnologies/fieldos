Privacy policy

ML Dynamic Solutions Private Limited · Last updated 6 November 2026

FieldOS stores the projects, reports, photos, attendance and other site records you create on your phone. If you sign in to Team & sync, those records, your email address and the name you entered are also stored in our cloud database (Supabase, region: Mumbai, India) so that the members of your company can see them. Only members of your company can read your company's data.

Location

Your location is read only when you punch a worker in or out, set a site location, or take a site photo with the in-app camera (the date, time and position are printed on the photo). It is saved with that record. It is never tracked in the background.

Voice reports

Voice reports use your phone's own speech recognition: Google's on Android and Apple's on iPhone. When the language's offline speech pack is on the phone, your speech is turned into text on the phone. Otherwise the phone's speech service may send it to Google or Apple to be recognised, under their privacy policies. FieldOS does not record or keep your voice.

Only if your phone can't recognise the language you chose, and your company owner has added a Sarvam key, FieldOS records your report and sends the recording to Sarvam AI (sarvam.ai) to be turned into text. FieldOS does not keep the recording afterwards.

Translating a report to English happens on the phone, using Google ML Kit. The language pack is downloaded from Google once, and the text of your report is never sent. ML Kit does send Google diagnostic details so that Google can maintain it: the phone model and system version, the app version, an identifier for this installation, the languages chosen, and how long translation took.

Maps

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. Their servers can see your device's IP address, as with any web request.

Payments

The FieldOS subscription is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle your card or UPI details. FieldOS never sees them. RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) receives the purchase record from the store so that the app can check your subscription.

Bug reports

If you use "Report a bug", we receive what you type, the email address you give (optional), the app version, your phone's operating system version and the app's last few error messages. No project data, photos or contacts are included. Reports are stored in our cloud database and copied to our private issue tracker on GitHub so that we can fix the problem.

Apart from what this policy describes, nothing about how you use FieldOS leaves your phone. We do not sell data or show ads.

Deleting data

In the app, open Team & sync and tap "Delete my cloud account". A company where you are the only member is deleted with all its records. In a company with other members, the records you added stay with that company, your name is removed from its team chat, and ownership passes to another member. You can also ask for deletion by emailing info@mldynamic.com. Uninstalling the app removes the data on the phone.
