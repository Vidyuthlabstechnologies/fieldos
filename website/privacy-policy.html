Privacy policy

ML Dynamic Solutions Private Limited · Last updated 11 November 2026

FieldOS stores the projects, reports, photos, attendance and other site records you create on your phone. If you sign in to Team & sync, those records, your email address and the name you entered are also stored in our cloud database (Supabase, region: Mumbai, India), so that the people your company works with can see what is meant for them.

Who can see your company's data

  • Members of your company see the company's sites and records, limited by their role. For example, only owners and project managers see salaries, leads and the change history.
  • Subcontractors, suppliers and the client see only what the company shares with them, and only after they join with a code an owner or project manager gave them. A subcontractor sees their own work orders and claims. A supplier sees the price requests and orders sent to them. A site's client sees that site's progress, its photos and the bills sent to them. They never see the rest of the company.
  • The FieldOS team does not look at company data, except to fix a problem you report or when the law requires it.

Location

Your location is read only when you check yourself in or out, punch a worker in or out, set a site location, or take a site photo with the in-app camera (the date, time and position are printed on the photo). It is saved with that record, so the company can see the work was done at the site. It is never tracked in the background.

Attendance photos and selfies

When staff check in or out, the app takes a selfie with the front camera and saves it with the time, the site and the GPS position. A supervisor can also take a photo when punching workers in. The photos are part of the company's attendance records: the owner and project managers of that company can see them, and they are used only to confirm attendance. FieldOS does not use face recognition.

Salaries, workers and leads

Companies use FieldOS to keep their own records about other people: workers' names, attendance and wages, staff salaries and advances, and leads (the name, phone number and enquiry of a possible customer). The company decides what it records and why. FieldOS stores and processes these records only on the company's behalf, and only owners and project managers can see salaries and leads.

Change history

When someone changes or deletes a money or progress record, FieldOS keeps who did it, when, and the old and new values, so the company can check its records. Owners, project managers and the accounts team can see this history.

Voice reports

Voice reports use your phone's own speech recognition: Google's on Android and Apple's on iPhone. When the language's offline speech pack is on the phone, your speech is turned into text on the phone. Otherwise the phone's speech service may send it to Google or Apple to be recognised, under their privacy policies. FieldOS does not record or keep your voice.

Only if your phone can't recognise the language you chose, and you are signed in to Team & sync, FieldOS records your report and sends the recording through its own server to Sarvam AI (sarvam.ai) to be turned into text. FieldOS does not keep the recording afterwards.

Translating a report to English happens on the phone, using Google ML Kit. The language pack is downloaded from Google once, and the text of your report is never sent. ML Kit does send Google diagnostic details so that Google can maintain it: the phone model and system version, the app version, an identifier for this installation, the languages chosen, and how long translation took.

AI assistant

When you use Ask FieldOS, what you type or say, and the site details the assistant needs to answer (such as project names, workers, tasks and amounts on this phone), go through FieldOS's server to Anthropic (anthropic.com), whose Claude model works out the reply. Anthropic's commercial terms do not let it use this to train its models. If Anthropic is unavailable, the same goes instead to Sarvam AI (sarvam.ai), which runs a DeepSeek model for the reply. FieldOS keeps only a count of how much each account uses the assistant each day and month, not the conversation. Nothing is saved to your records until you tap Save on the card the assistant shows.

Maps

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. Their servers can see your device's IP address, as with any web request.

Payments

The FieldOS subscription is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle your card or UPI details. FieldOS never sees them. RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) receives the purchase record from the store, linked to your FieldOS account id, so that the app and the assistant can check your subscription. Company plans are invoiced directly by ML Dynamic Solutions.

Bug reports and crash details

If you use "Report a bug", we receive what you type, the email address you give (optional), the app version, your phone's operating system version and the app's last few error messages. No project data, photos or contacts are included. Reports are stored in our cloud database and copied to our private issue tracker on GitHub so that we can fix the problem.

If the app crashes and "Send crash details" is on in Settings (you can turn it off at any time), the next time you are online the app sends the error, where in the app it happened, the app version and the phone's system version, the same way as a bug report. No project data is included.

If you use "Report this message" in project chat, we receive the reported message, the name it was sent under, the reason you picked, anything you add, and your account id. The sender is not told who reported it. Reports are stored in our cloud database and copied to our private issue tracker on GitHub so that we can review them within 24 hours. You can also block a teammate, which hides their chat messages on your phone.

Apart from what this policy describes, nothing about how you use FieldOS leaves your phone. We do not sell data or show ads.

How long we keep data

  • Company records stay as long as the company uses FieldOS, and are deleted with the account as described below.
  • Bug, crash and message reports are kept only as long as needed to fix or review them, and never more than 12 months.
  • Deleted data can remain in our hosting provider's automatic backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.

Deleting data

In the app, open Team & sync and tap "Delete my cloud account". A company where you are the only member is deleted with all its records and files: site photos, selfies, documents and claim photos. In a company with other members, the records you added (including your attendance) stay with that company, because they are its work records. Your name is removed from its team chat, ownership passes to another member, and your portal access and assistant usage are deleted. You can also ask for deletion by emailing info@mldynamic.com. Uninstalling the app removes the data on the phone. See Delete your account.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or complete it, ask us to erase it, and nominate someone to act for you. Email info@mldynamic.com from the address on your account. If your data is in a company's records (for example as a worker or a lead), we pass your request to that company, which decides about its own records, and we tell you that we have done so.

Grievance officer

For any complaint about your personal data, write to the Grievance Officer, ML Dynamic Solutions Private Limited, at info@mldynamic.com with "Privacy grievance" in the subject. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
