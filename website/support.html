Subscription and billing

  • FieldOS is bought through Google Play. New subscribers get a 3-day free trial.
  • To cancel, open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → FieldOS → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.
  • Got a new phone? Sign in to the same Google account and tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.
