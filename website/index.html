Features

Everything a site needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built for the supervisor in the sun with a basic Android phone, and for the owner who wants to know what happened on every site today.

Voice DPR in your language

Speak the day's work in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi or English. FieldOS writes it up, translates it to English and makes a clean PDF report.

GPS wage sheet

Punch workers in and out with their location, or mark the whole gang present in one tap. The wage sheet adds itself up at the end of the week.

Site expenses and balance

Log every payment, advance and bill against the site. See money in, money out and the balance on hand at a glance.

Material stock

Record what arrives, what is used and what is left, so you know before cement or steel runs out.

Stamped site photos

Every photo carries the date, time and GPS position printed on it. No more arguments about when work was done.

Every site, one view

Owners see all their sites from the office or from home. Your team syncs when it has signal; nothing is lost when it does not.

Approvals and checklists

Send purchases and over-budget items for approval. Run quality and safety checklists on site and keep the record.

Equipment logs

Track machine hours, fuel and breakdowns for JCBs, mixers and every hired machine.

Team chat with join codes

Add engineers and supervisors with a join code. Everyone talks in one place, tied to the right site.

Voice reports

Your supervisor talks. FieldOS writes the report.

Typing reports on site never happens. With FieldOS the supervisor holds the button and says what was done, in the language they think in. The app turns it into a clear daily progress report in English, ready to send to the client as a PDF.

Speech is recognised on the phone wherever the language pack is installed, so it works on sites with no network.

FAQ

Questions builders ask us

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything you enter is saved on the phone first. When the phone gets signal, FieldOS syncs with your company's cloud so the owner and office can see it.

Which phones does it run on?

Any Android phone from the last few years. An iPhone version is coming soon.

Which languages can my supervisors speak in?

Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi and English. Reports are translated to English on the phone, so the text of the report is not sent anywhere for translation.

How does the free trial work?

Pick the monthly (₹999 per person) or yearly (₹10,999 per person) plan in the app. The first 3 days are free. If you cancel in Google Play before the trial ends, you pay nothing.

Who can see my company's data?

Only members of your company. Cloud data is stored in Mumbai, India. We do not sell data or show ads. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Reports, wage sheets and other records can be shared as PDF, and you own every record you create.

How do I get help?

Use "Report a bug" inside the app, or email info@mldynamic.com. See the support page.
