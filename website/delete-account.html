Delete your FieldOS account

FieldOS by ML Dynamic Solutions Private Limited

You can delete your FieldOS cloud account and its data at any time, from the app or by email.

From the app

  1. Open FieldOS and go to Team & sync.
  2. Tap Delete my cloud account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email info@mldynamic.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my FieldOS account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, email address and name.
  • If you are the only member of your company: the company and all of its records (sites, reports, attendance, expenses and the rest), and every file stored for its sites: site photos, check-in selfies, documents and claim photos. Files are removed within minutes of the deletion.
  • If your company has other members: the records you added stay with that company, because they belong to its sites. That includes your attendance and check-in selfies, which are part of the company's attendance records. Your name is removed from its team chat, ownership passes to another member, and your supplier, client or subcontractor portal access and your AI assistant usage are deleted.
  • Bug reports you sent are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem, and never more than 12 months.
Uninstalling the app removes the data on the phone, but it does not delete your cloud account. Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
